What is The Superhuman Club?

A premium fitness and biohacking club by Muscleheadon, founded by Naveen Chandra Kulkarni. Every member is coached across six pillars — Workout, Nutrition, Supplements, Wellness, Clinical and Recovery — with the Mind at the centre connecting them all.

Where is The Superhuman Club?

In Bandra West, Mumbai (off Linking Road), in Bajaj Nagar, Nagpur, and online anywhere in India.

Who is Naveen Chandra Kulkarni?

The founder of The Superhuman Club by Muscleheadon. He has 12+ years in fitness, is ACE and Precision Nutrition certified, and has competed at Mr. Olympia in Las Vegas and the Dubai Muscle Show.

What is the Blueprint Assessment?

A free five-minute online assessment: 21 questions that score your six health pillars and your Mind, show your biggest lever, and email you a personal report.

What are the Pillar Protocols?

Fourteen-day protocol sheets, one per pillar, with step-by-step days, a daily tracker and a day-15 re-test. The Pillar Starter is ₹99 and each full sheet is ₹299.
