The Wellness Floor at The Superhuman Club by Muscleheadon is a private recovery and wellness facility in Bandra West, Mumbai. Guests book timed sessions online across five single-occupancy stations — infrared sauna, cold-water immersion, red-light therapy, steam and shower, and a recovery pod — run as a guided circuit by our wellness concierge.

Sessions are sold individually (single, dual or triple station circuits) and as prepaid session packs with fixed validity. All bookings are made and paid for on this website; payments are processed securely by Razorpay.
