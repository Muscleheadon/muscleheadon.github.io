Single sessions

  • Cancel or reschedule 24 hours or more before your slot: full refund to your original payment method, or a free reschedule.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or no-show: the session is forfeited and not refundable.
  • If we cancel a session (for example, equipment maintenance), you choose a full refund or a free reschedule.

Session packs

  • Unused packs can be cancelled for a full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided no credit has been used.
  • Once any credit is used, the pack is non-refundable. Credits expire at the end of the validity period shown at purchase.
  • Medical emergencies or relocation are reviewed case by case with valid proof.

How to cancel

Email muscleheadon@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 90825 15616 with your booking reference (SHC-…) and registered mobile number.

Refund timelines

Approved refunds are initiated within 2 working days and credited to your original payment method via Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days depending on your bank.

Payments made after a hold expires

If a payment completes after the 15-minute hold has expired and the slot is no longer available, we will refund it in full or offer an alternative slot.
