Muscleheadon Health & Fitness Private Limited operates this booking website. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Name, mobile number and email address you enter when booking.
  • Booking details: date, time, stations, pack credits and booking history.
  • Payment status and reference from Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are handled only by Razorpay and are never stored by us.
  • Health declarations collected at your induction, kept at the facility.

How we use it

  • To confirm, manage and remind you about bookings.
  • To keep your pack balance and booking history.
  • To contact you about your bookings and, if you agree, about offers. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • Google (Calendar), to schedule your station bookings.
  • Our hosting provider, to run this website.

We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep booking and payment records for as long as required for accounting and tax law, and other data only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing muscleheadon@gmail.com.
